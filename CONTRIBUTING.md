# Contributing

SCUM-SENF is early-stage. Issues, corrections, adapters, tests and focused pull requests are welcome.

Before submitting:

- keep the change small and explain the concrete problem it solves;
- cite primary sources for factual claims where practical;
- do not submit private server/player data, credentials or confidential material;
- identify copied material, its source and license;
- disclose material AI assistance;
- keep new dependencies and architectural complexity justified.

Contributions are accepted under **EUPL-1.2**. The submitting person remains responsible for the contribution and its sources.
